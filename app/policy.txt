ACME TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY

1. Domestic Airfare
Employees may claim economy-class airfare for approved domestic business travel.

2. Hotel Accommodation
Hotel accommodation is reimbursable up to INR 4,000 per night for domestic travel.

3. International Accommodation
International hotel accommodation is reimbursable up to INR 8,000 per night.

4. Meal Allowance
Employees may claim up to INR 1,200 per day for meals during approved domestic travel.

5. Claim Deadline
Travel expense claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days after returning from the trip.

6. Advance Booking
Domestic flights should be booked at least 7 days before departure unless there is an emergency business requirement.

7. Business Class
Business-class airfare is not reimbursable for domestic travel.

8. Supporting Documents
Employees must provide the invoice or receipt for hotel and meal reimbursement claims.
